The problem

You're not losing clients on service. You're losing them in your DMs.

Your front desk is excellent when they're free. The problem is they're rarely checking Instagram, and every unanswered DM is a client who books somewhere else within minutes.

/ 01

Staff can't reply to DMs while treating a client

Messages pile up unread during peak hours, precisely when your busiest, highest-value clients are trying to reach you.

/ 02

Most people don't message twice

If a DM goes unanswered the first time, most people don't follow up — they just message the next clinic on their feed instead.

/ 03

Replies that come hours later don't convert

By the time someone gets back to an Instagram enquiry, the client has often already booked elsewhere or lost interest entirely.
