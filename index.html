The problem

You're not losing clients on service. You're losing them on the phone.

Your front desk is excellent when they're free. The problem is they're rarely free, and every unanswered call is a client who books somewhere else within minutes.

01

Staff can't answer while treating a client

35 to 40% of calls go unanswered during peak hours, precisely when your busiest, highest-value clients are trying to reach you.

02

Most callers never try twice

Only 20 to 30% call back if they don't reach you the first time. The rest quietly become a competitor's client instead.

03

WhatsApp replies come too slowly to matter

By the time someone gets back to a WhatsApp enquiry, the client has often already booked elsewhere or lost interest entirely.
