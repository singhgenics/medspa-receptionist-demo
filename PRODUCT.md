# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners and managers of small to mid-size independent Dubai med spas and aesthetic clinics. Most run on Fresha or plain Google Calendar rather than enterprise platforms like Zenoti or Pabau, which already bundle their own AI receptionist features. They're evaluating whether to hire a solo automation consultant to stop losing bookings to missed calls and slow WhatsApp replies.

## Product Purpose

A demo and portfolio site, not a real client engagement, showing an AI receptionist that answers inbound WhatsApp messages and phone calls, checks calendar availability, and books confirmed appointments automatically. Used as a sales asset on Upwork and in direct outreach to Dubai med spas.

## Positioning

Every missed call is a booked appointment somewhere else. An AI receptionist that never misses a call or message, checks availability, and books the appointment on its own.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary and secondary CTA: message to get this built for your business is primary; see how it works (scroll to the explainer) is the secondary fallback.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: "Never miss another booking."
- Belief ladder: missed calls and slow replies cost real bookings → an AI receptionist can handle this reliably and naturally → it works with whatever you already book on, Google Calendar or Fresha → cheap relative to one recovered treatment booking → message to get started.
- Proof on hand: none yet, this is a pre-first-client demo. Credibility carried by the same builder's other completed automation work.

## Brand Personality

Calm, luxurious, trustworthy, warm. Should feel like a boutique wellness brand, not a tech dashboard. The automation itself should read as an excellent human receptionist, not a robotic AI.

## Anti-references

The generic "AI automation agency" template look. Cold, clinical medical-software aesthetics. Overly playful wellness clichés such as lotus flowers or cursive script overload.

## Design Principles

Show the real conversation, WhatsApp and voice call alike, as proof rather than ability-list bullets. Calm confidence over hype. The automation should feel invisible and natural, never robotic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Reduced-motion alternatives throughout for all scroll-reveal and hover-triggered animation.
